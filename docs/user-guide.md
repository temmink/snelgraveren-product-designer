# Snelgraveren Product Designer — User Guide

> This guide is for **customers** who use the designer on a WooCommerce product page to personalise products such as dog tags, name plates, baby plaques and serving boards.
>
> It describes version 1.12.8. Button names are given in English; the designer shows them in the shop's language (in Dutch, for example, **Add** is *Toevoegen* and **Edit** is *Bewerken*).

## Getting Started

The designer lets you personalise a product on its own product page: put your name on it, add a date or a message, upload a photo or logo, or pick a ready-made picture — and see the result before you order.

Not every product has the designer. When a product can be personalised you either see the designer on the page itself, or a **Customize Product** button.

---

## Opening the Designer

- **On a computer** the designer is usually part of the product page, a little below the product photo. A **Customize Product** button stays in the bottom-right corner of the screen for as long as the designer is out of sight; click it to jump there.
- **On a phone** the designer opens over the whole screen. Tap **Customize Product** — the button stays at the bottom of the screen while you scroll. **Close** brings you back to the product page; your design is kept.

Some shops use the full-screen version on computers as well. **Escape** closes it.

---

## What You See

- **The product** — the picture you are designing on. Anything you add appears here.
- **Size and colour** (only for products that come in several) — see [Choosing Size and Colour](#choosing-size-and-colour).
- **Front / Back** (only for products with more than one side).
- **Three tabs:**

| Tab | What it is for |
|-----|----------------|
| **Add** | Put a text, an image or clip art on the product. This is where the designer opens. |
| **Edit** | Change the element you selected: its text, font, size and colour. Opens by itself when you click an element. |
| **Layers** | A list of everything on this side of the product. Click a row to select that element — handy when two elements overlap and you cannot click the one underneath. |

- **Undo / Redo** — the two arrow buttons above the product.
- **Save & add to cart** — the blue button.

The product may show an outline marking where you can engrave or print. Some products keep your elements inside that outline; others let you move them further but hide whatever falls outside it.

---

## Choosing Size and Colour

When a product comes in several sizes or colours, you choose them **inside the designer**, right above the tabs. These are the same choices as on the product page — picking one in the designer picks it there too, and the other way round.

- **The picture follows your colour.** Choose *Red* and the product turns red, on every side.
- **Sold out** — an option that cannot be delivered in your current combination is listed as, for example, *Pink (sold out)* and cannot be chosen. If you change the size and your colour is not available in that size, the colour is cleared and the designer tells you why.
- You have to choose every option before you can order. If you forget, the designer asks for it and keeps your design.

---

## Front and Back

A product with more than one side shows the sides as buttons above the tabs — for example **Front** and **Back**. Click one to work on that side. What you made on the other side is kept.

Only the sides you have opened are part of your design. If you want something on the back, open **Back** at least once.

---

## Changing the Example Text

Many products start with an example on them — *Naam Hond*, *Voornaam*, a phone number. It shows where your own text will go.

1. Click the example on the product.
2. The **Edit** tab opens with the text in the **Your text** field.
3. Click in the field and type. The example is replaced by what you type.

You can also click the text on the product a second time and type there directly.

Some example texts are **fixed in place** by the shop: you can change the words, the font, the size and the colour, but you cannot move or remove them.

If you order while an example is still there, the designer warns you — see [Ordering](#ordering).

## Adding Text and Images

Open the **Add** tab.

### Add text

Click **Add text**. A new text appears on a free spot on the product, already selected. Type your own words in the **Your text** field, then drag the text where you want it.

### Add image

Click **Add image** and choose a file. Depending on the product you can use a photo (JPG, PNG, WebP) or vector artwork (SVG) — the button tells you which. Engraving-only products accept vector artwork only.

The shop may limit the file size or require a minimum picture size. If your file is refused, the message says what to change. A warning appears when a photo is enlarged so far that it would come out blurry.

### More kinds of text

Under **More kinds of text** you may find:

- **Curved text** — text along an arc. Offered only when the shop enabled it.
- **Engraving text** — single-line lettering for engraving and milling, where the machine follows each letter as one stroke.

For these two, click the button and **then click on the product** where the text should go. The designer reminds you.

## Editing an Element

Click an element on the product. The **Edit** tab shows:

| Setting | What it does |
|---------|--------------|
| **Your text** | The words themselves. |
| **Font** | The typeface. Shown when the shop offers more than one. |
| **Size** | The text size. The shop may set a minimum so the text stays readable when engraved. |
| **Color** | The colour of the text, when the shop allows a choice. |

Under **More options**:

| Setting | What it does |
|---------|--------------|
| **Style** | Bold and italic. |
| **Text alignment** | Left, centre or right — matters for text of several lines. |
| **Position on the product** | Move the element to the left, centre, right, top, middle or bottom of its area. |

**Delete** removes the element.

For a photo or clip art you may see a tint colour and, if the shop enabled them, image filters.

### Moving, resizing, rotating

- **Move** — drag the element.
- **Resize** — drag a corner.
- **Rotate** — drag the round handle above the element.

A shop can switch resizing or rotating off for a product.

---

## Using Clip Art

When the shop offers clip art you see **Clip Art** at the bottom of the **Add** tab, grouped in collections. Click a collection to open it, then click a picture to put it on the product. It behaves like any other element; whether you can recolour it depends on the product.

---

## Undo and Redo

The arrow buttons above the product undo and redo your last steps — adding, deleting, moving, and changes to text, font, size and colour. Undo stops at the product as it was when you started; it never removes the shop's own artwork.

---

## Ordering

Click **Save & add to cart**. Your design is saved and the product goes into your cart in one step. There is no separate save button.

Before it does, the designer checks three things and tells you if something is missing. Your design is kept in every case.

| Message | What to do |
|---------|------------|
| **First choose: Size, Colour** | Pick the options that are still empty — in the designer, or with **Choose options** on the product page. |
| **Sold out — choose another option** | The combination you picked cannot be delivered. Choose another size or colour. |
| **Still the example text: "…"** | An example is still on the product and would be made exactly like that. **Change it** selects it for you, **Leave it out** removes it and orders, **Order as it is** orders with the example on it. |

Some products must be personalised before they can be ordered at all.

### In your cart

- The product shows a picture of your design — every side you made.
- A "Design: Customized" label appears under the product name.
- If the shop charges for personalisation, the surcharge is added to the price.

After ordering, the designer on the product page starts fresh, so you can make another one.

---

## Changing a Design from Your Cart

1. Go to your cart and click the product name.
2. The product page opens with your design, your size and your colour filled in.
3. Change what you want and click **Save & add to cart** again.

This **updates** the item in your cart — you do not get a second one. If you want two of the same design, change the quantity in the cart.

### Starting from a design you ordered before

A design you have already ordered is kept exactly as it was made. You can open it again (for example from **My account → My designs**) and use it as a starting point: when you save, it becomes a **new** design, and the earlier order is not affected.

---

## On a Phone

- The designer fills the screen.
- The product stays at the top while you scroll through the options underneath.
- **Close** and **Save & add to cart** stay at the bottom of the screen.
- A selected element shows four small corner handles; they are easy to grab even though they look small.
- To change a text, tap it and use the **Your text** field — easier than typing on the product itself.
- Pinch-to-zoom is switched off while the designer is open, so your fingers move the design and not the page.

---

## Keyboard Shortcuts

| Shortcut | Action |
|----------|--------|
| **Ctrl+Z** / **Cmd+Z** | Undo |
| **Ctrl+Shift+Z** / **Cmd+Shift+Z** | Redo |
| **Delete** / **Backspace** | Remove the selected element (not while you are typing in a field) |
| **Escape** | Close the full-screen designer |
| **Tab / Shift+Tab** | Move between the controls; in the full-screen designer the focus stays inside it |

## Troubleshooting

### "Please customize your product before adding to cart"
This product has to be personalised. Open the designer and make your design first.

### "First choose: …"
The product comes in several sizes or colours and one is not chosen yet. Choose it in the designer, above the tabs.

### An option says "(sold out)"
That size or colour cannot be delivered in the combination you picked. Try another size — a colour sold out in one size may be available in another.

### "Still the example text: …"
See [Ordering](#ordering). It is a reminder, not an error; choose **Order as it is** if the text is really what you want.

### I cannot move or delete a text
The shop fixed it in place. You can still change its words, font, size and colour.

### I click an element but a different one gets selected
Two elements overlap. Open the **Layers** tab and click the one you want.

### My uploaded image was rejected
Read the message — usually the file is too large, the picture too small, or the file type is not accepted for this product.

### Elements won't move past a certain point
The product keeps your design inside the area that can be engraved or printed.

### I can't change the colour of a clip art item
The shop switched recolouring off for clip art on this product.

### "The server refused this request … the design is too large for the server's limits"
The shop's web server has a size limit on what it accepts in one request, and your design (usually because of a large photo) went over it. Try a smaller or more compressed photo, or let the shop know — the limit is set by their hosting, not by the designer.

### The designer won't load
- Make sure your browser is up to date (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge).
- Reload the page.
- Switch off browser extensions that block scripts or ads.

### I lost my design
A design is saved when you click **Save & add to cart**. If the shop cannot add it to the cart at that moment, the design is kept and shown again. A design you never saved is gone when you leave or reload the page.
